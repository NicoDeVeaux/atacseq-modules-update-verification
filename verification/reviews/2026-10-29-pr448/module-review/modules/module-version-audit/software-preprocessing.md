The most relevant untested follow-ups are:

- **Subread overlap boundaries.** Compare tiny BAM/SAF cases around the 0.2 threshold. A version bump also requires preserving paired-fragment counting: since 2.0.2, `-p` needs `--countReadPairs` to retain that meaning. Mixed SE/PE behavior is another reason the pin was retained. [Official notes](https://subread.sourceforge.net/).
- **Legacy MultiQC compatibility.** The real config repeats Picard sections, matching the broad trigger for [#2255, released in 1.20](https://github.com/MultiQC/MultiQC/pull/2255). Other pertinent upstream fixes include [MarkDuplicates parsing, 1.17](https://github.com/MultiQC/MultiQC/pull/2083) and [FastQC mean length, 1.25](https://github.com/MultiQC/MultiQC/pull/2817). These affect reporting; they do not by themselves establish wrong consensus count matrices.

Full refs, dates, actual command/config source links, applicability and limitations are in `software-preprocessing.json`. This was a bounded notes/source scan, not an exhaustive issue or optional-mode audit. No new runtime defect is claimed without reproduction. No frozen fix, pipeline source or cloud job was changed.
